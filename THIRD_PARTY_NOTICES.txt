Project assets

The repository includes Atoria-specific runtime sprites, textures, effects, and
museum models. Some of this art was generated with AI tools, including the
Atoria logo, background illustrations, skill icons, and museum surface texture.
Commercial Asset Store packages, original-game asset dumps, and imported audio
are outside the public source distribution.
